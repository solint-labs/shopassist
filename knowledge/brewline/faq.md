# FAQ

## Payments
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and popular wallets. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹3,000 in India.

## Gift cards
Digital gift cards are available from ₹500 to ₹10,000 and never expire. They can't be redeemed for cash.

## Decaf
Our decaf is a Colombian coffee decaffeinated with the Swiss Water Process, which uses no chemical solvents. It is 99.9% caffeine-free.

## Wholesale and cafés
We supply cafés and offices. Email wholesale@brewline.example for a price list and free samples.

## Account and login
You can log in with your email and a one-time code. We never ask for your password or OTP over chat.
