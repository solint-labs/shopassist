# Brewing guides

## Choosing a grind size
French press: coarse, like sea salt. Pour-over (V60, Chemex): medium-fine, like table salt. AeroPress: fine to medium-fine. Espresso: fine, like powdered sugar. South Indian filter: medium-fine.
If you're unsure, choose "whole bean" and grind just before brewing for the best flavour.

## Pour-over (V60) recipe
Use 15 g of coffee to 250 ml of water at 92-94°C. Bloom with 40 ml of water for 30-45 seconds, then pour slowly in circles to 250 ml. Total brew time should be 2:30-3:00 minutes.
If it tastes sour, grind finer. If it tastes bitter, grind coarser.

## French press recipe
Use 30 g of coarsely ground coffee to 500 ml of water just off the boil. Steep for 4 minutes, stir gently, then press slowly.

## Storing coffee
Keep beans in the resealable bag or an airtight container away from light, heat and moisture. Don't refrigerate coffee.
Coffee tastes best between 7 and 30 days after the roast date printed on the bag.
