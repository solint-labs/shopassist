# Returns and refunds

## Coffee beans and ground coffee
Coffee is a perishable food product, so opened bags cannot be returned.
Unopened bags can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund, minus the original shipping fee.
If you didn't enjoy a coffee, tell us. Every first order is covered by our Taste Promise: we'll send a different coffee of equal value once per customer.

## Brewing equipment
Grinders, kettles, drippers and other gear can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in the original packaging.
Equipment carries a 1-year manufacturer warranty against defects. Warranty claims need a photo or short video of the issue.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the item and the packaging.
We replace damaged or wrong items free of charge. You don't need to send damaged coffee back.

## How refunds work
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we approve the return.
Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer (NEFT) or as store credit, whichever you choose.

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are roasted. After roasting, the order can no longer be cancelled, but unopened coffee can be returned under this policy.
