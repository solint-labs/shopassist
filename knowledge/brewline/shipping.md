# Shipping

## Domestic shipping (India)
We roast to order every Monday, Wednesday and Friday and ship within 1 business day of roasting.
Delivery takes 2-4 business days to metro cities and 4-7 business days elsewhere in India.
Shipping is free on orders above ₹999. Below that, a flat fee of ₹79 applies.

## Express delivery
Express delivery (1-2 business days) is available in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad and Chennai for ₹149.
Orders placed before 12pm IST on a roast day go out the same day.

## International shipping
We ship to the UAE, Singapore, the UK and the USA. Delivery takes 7-12 business days.
International orders may be charged customs duties and import taxes by the destination country. These are paid by the customer on delivery.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking link by WhatsApp and email as soon as your order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Delayed or lost parcels
If your tracking hasn't updated for 5 business days, contact us and we will open an investigation with the courier.
If a parcel is confirmed lost, we reship it free of charge or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer.
