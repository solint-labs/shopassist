# Coffee subscriptions

## How subscriptions work
Choose a coffee (or "Roaster's Choice"), a grind size and a frequency: every 2, 3 or 4 weeks.
Subscribers save 10% on every delivery and always get free shipping.

## Pausing, skipping and changing
You can pause, skip a delivery, change your coffee or change the grind from the account page at any time up to 2 days before the next roast date.

## Cancelling a subscription
You can cancel anytime with no fees from your account page under Subscriptions > Manage.
Cancellations made at least 2 days before the next roast date apply to that delivery.
